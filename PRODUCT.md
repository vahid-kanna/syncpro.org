# Product Context: SyncPro

## One-Line Thesis
"SyncPro is the AI Project Controls Engineer that earns the right to update a construction schedule."

## Audience
- **Primary:** EPC Planning Heads, Project Controls Directors, and Chief Operating Officers (e.g. at Larsen & Toubro, Shapoorji Pallonji, Tata Projects, NCC, Bechtel).
- **Secondary:** Commercial Directors, Forensic Delay Claims Consultants, and Infrastructure Investment Panels (National Infrastructure Pipeline, MoSPI monitoring cells).

## Brand Personality & Tone
- **Forensic & Quantitative:** Real Critical Path Method (CPM), Total Float, Precedence Relationships (FS/SS), DCMA 14-Point benchmarks, FIDIC Clause 8.4 & 20.1, RERA Section 18 delay penalties.
- **Controlled & Non-Destructive:** We isolate the master Primavera P6 (.xer) / Asta (.pp) schedule. Qualified field evidence updates a parallel shadow graph, preserving audit integrity.
- **Tactile Luxury Engineering:** Velvet Obsidian surfaces (`#000000`, `#07080A`), Coral Orange Critical Path accent (`#FF6B35`), Solar Amber warning (`#F59E0B`), Luminous Emerald verification (`#10B981`).

## Anti-References (What SyncPro NEVER Looks Like)
- NO generic SaaS purple/indigo gradients.
- NO centered hero with three identical feature cards.
- NO vague marketing fluff ("supercharge your workflow", "scale effortlessly").
- NO fake illustrative SVG artwork pretending to be construction software.
- NO ungrounded AI claims without mathematical formulas or verifiable sector data (e.g. sourced MoSPI ₹4.92 Lakh Crore).
