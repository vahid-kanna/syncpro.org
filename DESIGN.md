# SyncPro Design System

## Overview
SyncPro is the category-defining AI Project Controls Engineer. The visual identity rejects generic SaaS tropes (purple gradients, centered 3-card marketing tiles, vague AI buzzwords). It marries the cold, unyielding precision of high-frequency quant trading terminals with the physical reality of civil infrastructure megaprojects.

## Surface-First Architecture
Every viewport strictly adheres to a functional surface archetype:
1. **Hero:** *Command & Operate* — An interactive P6 vs. Shadow CPM schedule reconciliation workbench.
2. **The Forensic Gap:** *Compare* — Rigorous side-by-side comparison between the deceptive monthly contractor PDF and real-time corroborated ground truth.
3. **Financial Exposure Sandbox:** *Simulate* — Real-time mathematical exposure modeling for Indian and global megaprojects.
4. **Capability Suite:** *Inspect* — Interactive tabbed workbench for Multimodal Ingestion, Shadow Scheduling, DCMA 14 Diagnostics, and FIDIC Dispute Defense.
5. **Validation & Pedigree:** *Certify* — Academic backing at Nirmaan, IIT Madras, and the 30-day read-only pilot protocol.

## Colors
- **Velvet Obsidian (#000000, #07080A, #0E1015):** Pure black foundation evoking institutional defense grade security.
- **Coral Orange (#FF6B35):** The Critical Path Backbone. Reserved strictly for primary calls to action, active critical paths, and brand identity anchors.
- **Solar Amber (#F59E0B):** Semantic warning indicator for near-critical float depletion and pending FIDIC time-bar deadlines.
- **Luminous Emerald (#10B981):** Semantic verification indicator for corroborated as-built logs, cryptographic hashes, and positive float.
- **Signal Crimson (#F87171):** Negative float alerts, critical path slippage, and unmitigated liquidated damages.

## Typography
- **Primary Display (Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans):** High legibility, tight tracking (-0.03em) for executive authority.
- **Editorial Serif (Newsreader Italic):** Warm human craftsmanship, used sparingly for philosophical emphasis.
- **Technical Monospace (JetBrains Mono):** Precision engineering tags, CPM node IDs, timestamps, and mathematical values.

## Motion & Tactility (Animmaster Standard)
- **Mouse Spotlight:** Dynamic radial luminescence tracks the cursor to softly highlight card borders.
- **Spring Physics:** Zero stiff mechanical transitions; all tabs, sliders, and modal dialogs utilize natural cubic-bezier physics.
- **3D CPM Topology (Three.js):** Real-time interactive 3D Critical Path graph with animated node energy pulses.
- **Reduced Motion Support:** All animations gracefully deactivate when `prefers-reduced-motion` is detected.

## Anti-Slop Discipline
- No generic indigo/violet AI glows.
- No decorative SVG illustrations pretending to be software.
- No arbitrary numbers without mathematical derivation.
- No dead `mailto:` buttons — every conversion path is backed by an interactive 3-step qualification console.
